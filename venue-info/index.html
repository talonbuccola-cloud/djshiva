Venue & Event Information

Planning to book DJ Shiva for a club, festival, or private event? The information below will help ensure the venue is prepared for a smooth setup and performance.

Download Technical Rider Request a Date

Equipment Provided by DJ Shiva

  • Pioneer DJ XDJ-RR all-in-one DJ system
  • Music media/USB drives
  • Headphones
  • Required power adapter
  • Audio cables for connecting to the venue’s sound system

Equipment Provided by the Venue or Event Organizer

  • A professional PA system appropriate for the venue and anticipated audience size
  • Main speakers and subwoofer(s)
  • An available XLR or RCA connection to the sound system
  • One sturdy DJ table or booth, preferably at least 4 feet wide and 2 feet deep
  • One grounded electrical outlet located near the DJ setup
The sound system should be set up and operational before DJ Shiva’s scheduled setup or sound-check time.

Outdoor Events

For outdoor events, the DJ table and equipment must be placed in a fully covered, dry area protected from rain and other weather conditions.

Need Help With Sound Equipment?

Not sure what equipment your venue provides? That’s okay. Let us know what is available when submitting your booking request, and we’ll help determine what may be needed.

When available, sound-equipment rental may be arranged. Any rental expense will be included as an additional cost in the event quote.
